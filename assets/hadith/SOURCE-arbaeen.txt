Matn of al-Arba'un an-Nawawiyya (the Forty Hadith of Imam an-Nawawi, d. 676 AH)

Rights: the text of the ahadith and of an-Nawawi's book (including his
takhreej lines) is centuries old and in the public domain. No modern edition's
tahqeeq, notes or numbering-specific apparatus is included.

How this file was made: the matn, tashkeel and takhreej were typed by hand
(tool/matn/*.json, merged by tool/build_arbaeen.dart). Nothing was copied from
an online dataset or a modern printed edition.

Status: DRAFT. The text has NOT yet been checked word by word against an old
printed edition. Before release, check every hadith against the chosen edition,
record that edition here and in README.md, then add
"checked": "<edition>" to that hadith in tool/matn/*.json and rebuild. The app
shows a draft notice on every hadith that is not checked yet.

Self-review (2026-11-04, interim, not a check against an edition):
all 42 matn and takhreej lines were re-read against an-Nawawi's known
wording, and scripts checked balanced quotes and missing harakat. No error was
found that could be fixed with certainty, so nothing was changed. These spots
must be settled against the printed edition (words differ between editions or
between the book and the Sahih collections):
- 23: "ما بين السماوات والأرض" or "ما بين السماء والأرض".
- 25: tashkeel of "وكل تكبيرة صدقة" and the two after it (genitive with
  accusative "صدقةً", or nominative "وكلُّ ... صدقةٌ").
- 29: "ويباعدني من النار" or "ويباعدني عن النار".
- 42: whether "ولا أبالي" also follows the second "غفرت لك".
No hadith is marked "checked" by this review.

Titles (the short headings in the index) are ours, not an-Nawawi's.
