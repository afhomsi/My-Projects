Short sharh (الشرح المختصر) for each of the 42 ahadith

Rights: written fresh for this app (tool/sharh/*.json, merged into
assets/hadith/arbaeen.json by tool/build_arbaeen.dart). It restates, in our
own words, meanings that are well known among the classical commentators of
the Forty (an-Nawawi, Ibn Daqiq al-'Eid, Ibn Rajab). No sentence was copied
from a printed or online sharh, classical or modern. Ibn 'Uthaymeen's and
al-Khudayr's shuruh were not used. The app may use this text freely; it is
offered under CC BY 4.0 like the rest of the app's own content.

Status: DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a qualified student of knowledge. The app
shows a draft notice under every sharh that is not reviewed. After review, add
"reviewed": "<name of reviewer>" to that entry in tool/sharh/*.json and run
dart run tool/build_arbaeen.dart.

Longer quoted shuruh (the "more" section of v1) come later, only from old
editions of free classical works, each with its own source line.
